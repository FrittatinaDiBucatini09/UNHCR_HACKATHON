## Organizations and stated involvement

- **University of Trento (UNITN):** co-leads the initiative with UNHCR; supplies academic organizers, speakers, and facilitators.
- **UNHCR Innovation / UNHCR Innovation Service:** co-leads the initiative; supplies the challenge framing, speakers, data/context Q&A, and responsible-use guidance.
- **UNHCR MCO Italy:** Saverio Ojetti's listed affiliation.
- **Speck&Tech:** community associated with the Day 0 public event and networking.
- **Impact Hub Trento:** Day 0 venue.
- **Spazio ZERO:** Thursday-Friday hackathon venue.
- **Afliant:** supplies technical mentors, the Day 2 partner spotlight, at least one jury member, and the Day 1 pizza dinner.
- **Fondazione HIT:** Milena Bigatto's affiliation and part of the Day 1 partner moment.
- **CoderDolomiti:** Coffee Station and community/organizational affiliation through Milena Bigatto.
- **Glow Association:** founded by Francesca Fiore; listed as part of her affiliation.
- **Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele:** Piero Ronzani's affiliation.
- **Centro per la Cooperazione Internazionale (CCI):** Giorgio Comai's affiliation.
- **Internazionale:** shown among partner logos on the UniTrento listing; the dedicated site promises a one-year subscription as the winning team's prize.

## Name/role boundaries

- The affiliation page lists **Daniele Miorandi** and **Giorgio Comai** explicitly as jury members, but does not say they are the complete jury.
- The timed schedule names **Breno Elias Valentini and Saverio Ojetti** together under “UNHCR Innovation” for the Q&A, while the affiliation page more specifically places Saverio Ojetti at **UNHCR MCO Italy**. Both statements are preserved; the timed label may describe event representation rather than formal employment unit.
