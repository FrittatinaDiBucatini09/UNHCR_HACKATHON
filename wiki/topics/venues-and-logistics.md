# Venues and logistics

| When | Venue | Address |
|---|---|---|
| Wednesday 30 Sep 2026 | Impact Hub Trento | Via Roberto da Sanseverino 95, 38122 Trento |

The Day 1 registration line repeats the hackathon address as Via Salè 1, Povo.

## Access and audience

- Day 0 is a public evening held in English.
- Entry to Day 0 is free but ticketed.
- A Day 0 livestream is available through the event listing.
- The PDF does not print the event-listing, registration, or livestream URL. The [[sources/speckandtech-ticket-page|linked ticket page]] gives [Day 0 tickets](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/speckandtech/2372897) and a [livestream link](https://tinyurl.com/speck-live), which redirects to the Speck&Tech YouTube live channel. Stream availability was not confirmed. The ticket page showed a waiting-list option when checked on 2026-10-01.
- The Day 2 Pitch Clinic is restricted to team representatives.
- The dedicated site says registrations for the hackathon are closed as observed on 2026-10-01. The UniTrento listing states a registration deadline of 2026-09-26 at 23:59. This is separate from the Day 0 public event's ticketing.
- The event sites state that the hackathon is held in English. The dedicated site lists `giacomo.scettri@unitn.it` as a contact for questions.

## Food and breaks

- Day 1, 11:30-12:00: Coffee break and CoderDolomiti Coffee Station opening, with a partner moment involving Milena Bigatto, CoderDolomiti, and Fondazione HIT.
- Day 1, 13:00-14:00: Informal lunch at or near the hackathon venue.
- Day 1, 19:00-20:00: Informal pizza dinner at the hackathon venue, offered by Afliant.
- Day 2, 13:00-14:00: Lunch break; no location, format, or sponsor is specified.

## Daily timing

- Day 0 public event: 20:30-23:45.
- Day 1 participant registration begins at 08:30; the final sprint ends and the venue closes at 22:00.
- Day 2 begins at 09:00 and ends with presentations, jury deliberation, and awards by 20:00.
