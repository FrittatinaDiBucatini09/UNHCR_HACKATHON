# Participation and prizes

## Who can join

The [[sources/official-hackathon-site|dedicated UniTrento site]] says the hackathon is open to students across the UniTrento community, including students without technical backgrounds. It states there are no technical prerequisites, though data-analysis experience may help. The [[sources/unitn-event-listing|event listing]] says the hackathon is held in English.

Applicants may join with a pre-formed team or apply individually. The organizers say they will match individual applicants into multidisciplinary teams.

## Registration and contact

The UniTrento listing gives a registration deadline of **2026-09-26 at 23:59**. The dedicated site said **registrations are closed** when checked on 2026-10-01. The listed contact for questions is `giacomo.scettri@unitn.it`. The separately ticketed 30 September public event is described in [[topics/programme|the programme]].

## Prize

The dedicated site states that the winning team receives a **one-year subscription to *Internazionale***. It does not establish whether there are other prizes. The separate [[sources/cashy-oversight-challenge-site|Cashy challenge site]] and [[sources/cashy-oversight-challenge-faq|FAQ]] publish a four-part judging rubric; see [[topics/cashy-submission|submission and judging]].
