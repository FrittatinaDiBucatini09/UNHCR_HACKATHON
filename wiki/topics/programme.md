# Detailed programme

Times are presented as local event times in Trento, Italy; the PDF does not print a timezone identifier. Activity titles and talk titles are preserved in English as printed.

## Day 0 - Wednesday 30 September 2026

**Theme:** Open the conversation  
**Public opening event:** *Speck&Tech #88 - AI in Humanitarian Action*  
**Time:** 20:30-23:45  
**Venue:** Impact Hub Trento, Via Roberto da Sanseverino 95

The English-language public evening explores how AI is already used in humanitarian action and what fairness, transparency, explainability, and human oversight mean when the stakes are high. It opens the broader three-day initiative led by the University of Trento in collaboration with UNHCR.

**Format:** Two talks on data, AI, and humanitarian action, followed by audience discussion and Speck&Tech community networking. Entry is free but ticketed. A livestream is available through the event listing.

**Speakers and talks:**

- **Breno Elias Valentini**, Associate Innovation Officer, UNHCR Innovation Service - *How Humanitarian Organizations are using AI: opportunities, risks, and emerging practice*.
- **Piero Ronzani**, postdoctoral researcher, Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele, Milan - *Data at Work for Vulnerable Lives*.

The linked [[sources/speckandtech-ticket-page|Speck&Tech ticket page]] uses a different title for Breno's talk, *From responsible AI to humanitarian impact: designing digital solutions for refugee response*. The PDF title above remains the PDF's claim; the final title needs organizer confirmation.
