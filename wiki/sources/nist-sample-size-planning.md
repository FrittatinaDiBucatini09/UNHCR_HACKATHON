# Source: NIST sample size planning

NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods, [Selecting Sample Sizes](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/ppc/section3/ppc333.htm), checked 2026-10-01.

The guidance connects sample size to the parameter being estimated, desired precision, prior variability and practical cost. It discusses stratification and approximate proportion sample-size calculations. These are general statistical methods, not evidence for a Cashy-specific audit percentage or number of reviewers. Clustered repeated assessments and repeated monitoring require an appropriate extension rather than direct use of an independent-binomial formula.

Used in [[topics/cashy-pipeline-planning|pipeline planning]] to distinguish literature-informed methods from project-specific sample-size calculation. No numerical audit setting has been adopted.
