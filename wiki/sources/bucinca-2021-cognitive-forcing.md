# Source: Buçinca et al 2021 cognitive forcing

Buçinca, Malaya and Gajos, *To Trust or to Think: Cognitive Forcing Functions Can Reduce Overreliance on AI in AI-assisted Decision-making*, ACM CSCW / Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction (2021), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3449287), [author preprint abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.09692).

Checked 2026-10-01: author-submitted abstract and publication identification; full methods and numerical results have not yet been reviewed. This is a preliminary bibliographic source, not a completed full-text ingest.

The abstract reports an experiment with N=199: cognitive forcing reduced overreliance relative to simple explainable-AI approaches, but the most effective designs received the least favorable subjective ratings. Benefits varied with participants' motivation for effortful cognition. The task and participant population must be examined before transferring conclusions to humanitarian professionals or long-term use.
