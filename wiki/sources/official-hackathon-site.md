## Content relevant to the wiki

- Joint initiative of the University of Trento and UNHCR. Open to the UniTn student community; intended work uses real-world data and evidence for vulnerable populations in sub-Saharan Africa. The site stresses technical expertise, social awareness, and ethical data use.
- Event: 30 September–2 October 2026 in Trento. Hackathon activities: 1–2 October at Spazio ZERO, Via Salè 1, Povo. A public dissemination event takes place on 30 September.
- Students from all backgrounds may apply. No technical prerequisites; some data-analysis experience is helpful. Applicants may enter as pre-formed teams or individually; individual applicants are matched into teams.
- The winning team is promised a one-year subscription to *Internazionale*.
- The page states that registrations are closed and gives `giacomo.scettri@unitn.it` for questions.
- Mentors named: Breno Elias Valentini, Saverio Ojetti, Giacomo Scettri, Michele Tizzoni.
- Organizing committee named: Giacomo Scettri, Michele Tizzoni, Alberto Montresor, Milena Bigatto, Francesca Fiore.
