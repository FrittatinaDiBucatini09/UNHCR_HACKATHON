# Source: Green 2022 institutional oversight

Ben Green, *The Flaws of Policies Requiring Human Oversight of Government Algorithms*, Computer Law & Security Review 45 (2022), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clsr.2022.105681), [author preprint abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.05067).

Checked 2026-10-01: publication metadata and abstract; full policy analysis has not yet been reviewed. Preliminary bibliographic source.

The paper examines 41 policies and challenges the assumption that human involvement necessarily supplies effective oversight. It proposes institution-level justification and evaluation of algorithm adoption and oversight. It is a policy analysis rather than an experiment demonstrating a particular Cashy interface intervention.
