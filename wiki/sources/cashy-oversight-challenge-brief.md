## Distinct detail beyond the webpage summary

- Held-out real model test set: 1,828 class-balanced records. Score mean absolute error 0.03 points; inclusion recall 82%, exclusion recall 53%.
- The study had 155 assessments from 31 caseworkers (five cases each), 13 in plain AI and 18 in the statement arm.
- S8 is explicitly the data supplied for this challenge. It has the same SHA-256 as the CSV linked from the webpage; its frequencies are not those of the real operation.
- The brief's proposed deliverables are a repository/README, five-minute demo and two-page note. See [[topics/cashy-submission|submission]] for the FAQ's qualification that final requirements are to be shared during the event.
