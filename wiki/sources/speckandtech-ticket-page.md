# Source: Speck&Tech 88 ticket page

## Provenance

- Title: **Speck&Tech 88 “AI in humanitarian action”**.
- URL: [Ticket Tailor event listing](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/speckandtech/2372897).
- Linked from the [[sources/official-hackathon-site|dedicated UniTrento site]] and checked on 2026-10-01. Live web page, not an immutable copy.

## Event and access

- Wednesday 30 September 2026, 20:30–23:45 at Impact Hub Trentino, Via Roberto da Sanseverino 95, Trento.
- English-language event; valid ticket required for entry and scanned at the door. The page displayed a waiting-list option when checked.
- The page provides a [livestream link](https://tinyurl.com/speck-live), which redirects to the [Speck&Tech YouTube live channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCXCuc526ehOeTRusRrYnqVw/live). Stream availability was not confirmed.
- Speck&Tech describes itself as Trentino's digital community, active since 2016 and hosting free technology events. This evening opens the broader UniTrento and UNHCR initiative.

## Speakers

- **Breno Elias Valentini:** the ticket page labels his talk *From responsible AI to humanitarian impact: designing digital solutions for refugee response*. Its abstract concerns real UNHCR data, digital, and AI projects, their opportunities and risks, and accountability, transparency, and human impact in deployment. The page identifies him as an Associate Innovation Officer at UNHCR working on digital innovation projects.
- **Piero Ronzani:** the ticket page labels his talk *Data at work for vulnerable lives*. Its abstract mentions research on conflict and mental health in Gaza and the West Bank, a climate vulnerability index for refugee households in Jordan, and large-language-model analysis of rural radio broadcasts in Kenya. The page describes him as a behavioral and development economist and a postdoctoral researcher at Università Vita-Salute San Raffaele, with research affiliations in Berlin.

## Cross-source discrepancy

The [[sources/unhcr-unitn-hackathon-programme|advanced PDF programme]] gives Breno's talk a different title: *How Humanitarian Organizations are using AI: opportunities, risks, and emerging practice*. The event time, venue, and speakers align. The two titles are preserved as source-specific claims; the final title is unconfirmed.
