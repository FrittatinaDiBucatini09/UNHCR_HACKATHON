## Clarifications

- Improving the model is optional. Software is not mandatory, nor must a solution be production-ready. Governance, oversight processes, design and evaluation frameworks can qualify.
- External datasets may be used if documented and free of privacy, security, licensing and ethical problems. Generative AI may be used with disclosure, limitations and human accountability.
- Final submission requirements will be shared during the event; teams should expect a prototype/concept/framework, short pitch, documentation and impact-measurement explanation.
- Approximate expected attendance is 50 participants, with mentors and dedicated UNHCR Q&A.
