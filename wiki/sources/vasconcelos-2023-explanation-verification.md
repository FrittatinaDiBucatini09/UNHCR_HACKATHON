# Source: Vasconcelos et al 2023 explanation verification

Vasconcelos, Jörke, Grunde-McLaughlin, Gerstenberg, Bernstein and Krishna, *Explanations Can Reduce Overreliance on AI Systems During Decision-Making*, Proceedings of the ACM on Human-Computer Interaction / CSCW (2023), [publication DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3579605), [authors' Stanford laboratory page](https://cicl.stanford.edu/publication/vasconcelos2023explanations/).

Checked 2026-10-01: authors' publication page and abstract; full methods have not yet been reviewed. This is a preliminary bibliographic source.

For the guided-check selection question, rechecked the [author-submitted abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.06823), revised 2023-01-26. Access remains abstract-only, not a completed full-text review.

Five maze-task studies (combined N=731) varied costs and benefits of engaging with explanations. The authors find conditions in which explanations reduce overreliance and argue that some ineffective explanations may fail to reduce verification costs sufficiently. This supports investigating ease of checking a recommendation, but it does not establish effectiveness for Cashy, professional caseworkers or repeated use over time.
